WINDOWS

trio · 3 performersrunning time · 20 minpremiered · 2025

What if light didn't reveal — but confined?

Inspired by Plato's allegory of the cave, WINDOWS places three dancers in windows of light cut out of the black stage. Each moves within their frame, prisoner of what they believe they see — until the windows shift, cross, and open. François Kaleka's lighting scenography is an integral part of the choreography: here, light doesn't show the dance, it dances with it.

Director's note

Our screens have become our windows: frames of light that claim to show the world and end up replacing it. WINDOWS brings that tension to the stage — three bodies, three frames, and the question of what we choose to look at.
