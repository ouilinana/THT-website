A DUET BY OUILI NANA & RAPHAËL SAINT-LAURENT
duet · 2 performersrunning time · 50 minstage & open air

The search for meaning and joy in a world in flux.

The title plays on “search with the body” and the echo of “encore” — exploration and continual return. Through linked vignettes, the dancers move between tension and release, connection and solitude, fragility and resilience. These contrasts mirror our inner contradictions while hinting at the potential for harmony.

At its heart, the piece asks how we coexist despite our differences, and how individual quests can align with collective rhythms. It embraces uncertainty and invites us to find meaning not in fixed answers but in the shared act of moving — together and apart.
