The company
OUILI NANA
artistic direction · choreography · performance

Trained with Black Blanc Beur and Yeah Yellow, a bboy first, he performed from the US to Egypt before founding THT. First prize for choreography at the Theater Without Fund festival (Alexandria).

RAPHAËL SAINT-LAURENT
artistic direction · choreography · performance

A bboy shaped by contemporary dance and somatic practice, he questions tradition through an aesthetic and philosophical inquiry of the body.

FRANÇOIS KALEKA
lighting design · scenography

Together with Tony Cordella and Tessa Raguel (performers, WINDOWS), he completes a team where light is part of the choreography.
