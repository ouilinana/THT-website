A WORK BY OUILI NANA

FLEUR

solo · 1 performerrunning time · 50 minphotos · LeDockerNoir

A spellbinding journey where grace meets raw performance.

Inseparable from the time it dances with, beauty endlessly veils and unveils itself. FLEUR tells this story through the language of the body and hip-hop dance. Like a flower feeding on its environment, beauty draws on our desires and our subjectivity, building a path where its movements open — until it fully blooms. Yet the flower, like beauty, so perfect in our eyes, remains ephemeral and fragile — to neglect, and to its own ecosystem.

Ouili Nana

Born into a family of artists, Ouili discovered hip-hop dance in 2011 in Carrières-sous-Poissy. Trained with Black Blanc Beur and Yeah Yellow, a floor-focused bboy who also moves standing, he performed from the US to Egypt before turning to choreography. In Alexandria, at the Theater Without Fund festival, the first draft of FLEUR won him his first prize.
